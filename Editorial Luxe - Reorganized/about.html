The Label

Unspoken by design

UNTKN started in a single room in Kolkata with one screen, one squeegee and a drawer of drawings nobody had asked for. We print in runs of a few dozen. Every graphic is drawn by hand before it is separated for screen, and once a run is gone it does not come back. Nothing here is designed to be explained — only worn.

Drawn by hand

Every graphic starts as ink on paper in the studio. Nothing is licensed, nothing is traced.

Heavyweight only

Waffle knits and brushed cottons chosen for weight and drape, cut for a relaxed, boxy fit.

Short runs

Each piece is produced in a small batch, sold once, and never reprinted.
